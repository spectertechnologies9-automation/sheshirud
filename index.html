About Me

Leadership with a Purpose

Solving real-world mobility challenges, one commute at a time.

Sheshirud M M is the Managing Director of TaxityFleet Pvt. Ltd., and a leader with a genuine passion for solving real-world mobility challenges.

Working at the meeting point of transportation, technology and business, he saw a growing need for employee transportation that is reliable, efficient and enabled by technology — and set about helping businesses meet it.

Under his leadership, TaxityFleet continues to grow as a dependable partner for organisations seeking seamless employee commute solutions, pairing innovation with operational excellence to support workforce productivity and well-being.
